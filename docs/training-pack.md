# Training pack: Federal Ministry of Transportation website admin

> Generated from the in-app Help guides by `npm run docs:build`. Do not edit by hand.

This pack has everything a trainer needs to teach the website admin, and everything a person needs to practise afterwards. The same guides are inside the admin under **Help**, searchable and printable.

## How to run the training

- Group people by what they will do (the five sessions below). Run each session at a computer with the admin open, with a **practice** account and the training copy of the site, never the live one.
- For each guide: the trainer does it once while people watch, then everyone does it themselves. People learn by doing the guide's steps.
- End every session with the **practice tasks** in the UAT script (`uat-script.md`) for that role. Someone who completes them unaided is ready.
- Leave each person with the printable **quick reference** (Help → Quick reference) for their role.

## Session 1: Getting started

**For:** Everyone who will use the admin · **Time:** about 45 minutes

**By the end:** Everyone can sign in, find their way around, understand when changes reach the website, and know where to get help.

**Guides in this session:**

- Accept your invitation and sign in (3 minutes)
- Find your way around the admin (3 minutes)
- How changes reach the website
- Change your password (2 minutes)
- Reset a password you have forgotten (3 minutes)
- Common problems and quick answers

### Accept your invitation and sign in

*Everyone · about 3 minutes*

Choose your password the first time, then sign in whenever you need to update the website.

1. Open the invitation email and click the link in it.
   - The email says you have been invited to manage content on the Ministry's website. The link works once and expires after 72 hours.
2. On the Activate your account page, type a password in “Password”, then type it again in “Confirm password”.
   - Use at least 12 characters. A short sentence that only you would think of works well and is easy to remember.
3. Press “Activate account”.
   - You are signed in and taken to your dashboard.
4. Next time, go to the admin address you were given, type your email in “Email” and your password in “Password”, then press “Sign in”.

**Good to know**
- Your sign-in lasts 12 hours. After that you sign in again. The admin warns you 15 minutes before it ends.
- Keep your password private. No one from the Ministry or the support team will ever ask for it.

**If something goes wrong**
- *The page says Invitation expired.* Invitation links work once and last 72 hours. Ask a super administrator to open “Users & access”, choose “Manage” next to your name, and press “Send a new invitation”.
- *I never received the email.* Check your spam or junk folder first. If it is not there, ask a super administrator to send a new invitation. If email is not set up yet, they can give you the link directly.

### Find your way around the admin

*Everyone · about 3 minutes*

What each part of the screen is for, and what you can and cannot see.

1. Look at the menu on the left (on a phone, press “Menu” at the top).
   - It lists the parts of the website you are allowed to change under Content. Super administrators also see Administration, with “Users & access”, “Activity log” and “System status”.
2. Press “Dashboard” at any time to come back to the start page. (`/admin`)
3. Open any section. Each one shows a list with the tabs “Published” and “Archived”.
   - Published items are on the website now. Archived items are hidden but never deleted. The tabs “Drafts” and “Waiting for approval” appear when something is in them.
4. Press the green Add button at the top of a list to create something new, or “Edit” beside an item to change it.
5. At the bottom of the menu you will find “Help”, “My account”, “View website” and “Sign out”. (`/admin/account`)
   - “View website” opens the public site in a new tab so you can check your work.

**Good to know**
- To find an item in a long list, type a word in the search box above the list and press “Search”. It looks in titles and text. “Clear search” shows everything again.
- You only see the sections a super administrator has given you. If something is missing, ask them.
- If you can publish in any section, “Approvals” appears in the menu with a number: the items waiting for your decision.
- A section you are not allowed to open takes you back to the dashboard with a short notice.

### How changes reach the website

*Everyone*

When your changes go live, when they wait for a publisher, how to check them, and why nothing is ever lost.

1. Make your change and press the save button (for example “Save changes”).
   - If you can publish in that section, the change is live on the public website straight away.
2. If you can edit but not publish, your work is kept as a draft, or as proposed changes to something already published. Press “Submit for approval” when it is ready.
   - Nothing reaches the website until a publisher approves it. The buttons tell you which kind of person you are: “Save as draft” and “Submit for approval” mean your work is reviewed first.
3. To check it, press “View on website” on the item's page, or “View website” in the menu.
   - Both open the public site in a new tab. If you still see the old version, refresh the tab.
4. To take something off the website, a publisher presses “Archive”.
   - Archiving hides it immediately. It is not deleted, and it can be brought back with “Restore”.

**Good to know**
- Changing the order of a list only takes effect when you press “Save new order”.
- The “Activity log” (super administrators) records who changed what, and when.

### Change your password

*Everyone · about 2 minutes*

Choose a new password while you are signed in.

1. Press “My account” in the menu. (`/admin/account`)
2. In the Change password panel, type your present password in “Current password”.
3. Type the new one in “New password” and again in “Confirm new password”.
   - At least 12 characters. A short sentence works well.
4. Press “Change password”.

**Good to know**
- Changing your password signs you out of any other browser you were using. You stay signed in here.

### Reset a password you have forgotten

*Everyone · about 3 minutes*

Get an emailed link and choose a new password.

1. On the sign-in page, click “Forgot your password?”.
2. Type your email address in “Email” and press “Send reset link”.
3. Open the email and click the link.
   - The link works once and lasts 60 minutes. The page always says the same thing whether or not the address has an account, so check you typed it correctly.
4. On the Choose a new password page, type a new password in “New password” and again in “Confirm new password”.
5. Press “Change password”, then sign in with the new password.

**Good to know**
- If you can still sign in and just want a different password, use “My account” instead (see the guide Change your password).

**If something goes wrong**
- *No email arrives.* Check your spam folder, wait a few minutes, then try once more. If it still does not arrive, ask a super administrator to check “System status”.
- *The page says Link expired.* Reset links last 60 minutes and work once. Start again from “Forgot your password?”.

### Common problems and quick answers

*Everyone*

The things people most often get stuck on, and what to do.

1. Find your problem below, and follow the answer. If it does not help, tell a super administrator exactly what you pressed and what the screen said.

**If something goes wrong**
- *I cannot sign in.* Check your email address and password, then use “Forgot your password?” if needed. After several wrong attempts the admin makes you wait for a few minutes. If your account was deactivated, ask a super administrator.
- *A section I need is missing from the menu.* You only see sections you have been given. Ask a super administrator to add it to your access.
- *I saved, but the website looks the same.* If you can publish in that section, saved changes go live straight away: refresh the website tab, then check you edited the right item, that it is not archived, and (for news) that its Kind is the one you meant. If you can only edit, your work is a draft or a proposal until a publisher approves it: open the item, and press “Submit for approval” if you have not yet.
- *I cannot see “Archive” or “Restore” or the arrows to re-arrange.* Those change what is on the website, so they need the publish permission for the section. Ask a publisher, or ask a super administrator to give you “Publish” in “Users & access”.
- *I submitted something and nobody has approved it.* Open the section and check the “Waiting for approval” tab. Only people who can publish in that section can approve it: tell one of them, or ask a super administrator who they are. If a note says it was sent back, open the item and read it.
- *My change was refused with 'Please correct the highlighted fields'.* Look for a field marked in red, fix it, and press save again. What you typed is kept.
- *It says someone else changed the item.* Reload the page, then make your change again. See the guide When 'Someone else changed this item' appears.
- *A picture will not upload.* Use JPG, PNG, WebP or AVIF under 8 MB. For documents use PDF, Word or Excel under 25 MB. See the guide Upload pictures and files.
- *The page asks if I want to leave.* You have changes that are not saved yet. Stay, press save, then leave.
- *A banner says my session is ending.* Save your work. If it ends first, press “Sign in again in a new tab”, sign in there, then come back to your page and press save. Nothing you typed is lost.
- *I cannot archive the last hero slide.* The home page needs at least one slide. Add a new one first, then archive the old one.

### Publish a news story or press release

*Anyone who can edit “News, Photo News & Events” · about 10 minutes*

Add an online news report or a press release to the website.

1. Press “News, Photo News & Events” in the menu, then “Add item”. (`/admin/news/new`)
2. Set “Kind” to “Online news (Online News Sector)”.
3. Type the “Title” and choose the “Date”.
   - The date is the publication date. It starts as today.
4. If it is a press release, tick “This is a press release”.
   - It is then labelled Press Release instead of Online News Report.
5. Write the “Short summary”.
   - It appears on cards and lists, so keep it to a sentence or two.
6. Paste the story into “Full text”.
   - Leave a blank line between paragraphs. Plain text only: no bold, bullets or pictures inside the text.
7. Add a “Picture” with “Upload a picture”, and describe it in “Picture description (for screen readers)”.
   - If you skip the picture, the standard picture for this kind is used.
8. To show more pictures under the story, press “Add a picture” under “More pictures”.
   - Upload each one, describe it, and add a caption if you like. Use “Up”, “Down” and “Remove” to arrange them.
9. Tick “Feature this item” if it should be highlighted on the home page.
10. Press “Create item” if you can publish, or “Save as draft” if you cannot.
   - You are taken to the item's page. A publisher's story is already live. A draft is not on the website: press “Submit for approval” when it is ready.

**Good to know**
- Check it with “View on website” on the item's page.
- A date in the future still shows straight away: there is no scheduling.
- To correct a story later, open it from the list with “Edit” and press “Save changes”.

### Publish photo news

*Anyone who can edit “News, Photo News & Events” · about 8 minutes*

Add a photo story to the Photo News page.

1. Press “News, Photo News & Events”, then “Add item”. (`/admin/news/new`)
2. Set “Kind” to “Photo news”.
3. Type the “Title”, choose the “Date”, and write the “Short summary”.
4. Add the main photo under “Picture” with “Upload a picture”, and describe it in “Picture description (for screen readers)”.
5. Press “Add a picture” under “More pictures” for each extra photo.
   - For each one, upload the file and fill in “Picture description (for screen readers)” and “Caption (optional)”. They appear as a gallery under the story, two to a row.
6. If the story needs words, add them in “Full text”.
7. Press “Create item”.

**Good to know**
- A gallery can hold up to 24 pictures. Every picture needs a description for screen readers.
- Put the best photo first: use “Up” and “Down” to arrange the gallery, and “Remove” to take one out.

### Add an event

*Anyone who can edit “News, Photo News & Events” · about 5 minutes*

List an upcoming or past event on the Events page.

1. Press “News, Photo News & Events”, then “Add item”. (`/admin/news/new`)
2. Set “Kind” to “Event”.
3. Type the “Title” and choose the “Date” of the event itself.
4. Type the “Location”.
5. Write the “Short summary”: when it starts, the theme, who is attending.
6. Press “Create item”.

**Good to know**
- Events have no full text and cannot be featured. The latest events also appear in the Latest News & Events section on the home page.

### Change or add a home page hero slide

*Anyone who can edit “Home page hero” · about 5 minutes*

Update the big pictures and headlines at the top of the home page.

1. Press “Home page hero” in the menu. (`/admin/hero`)
2. To change a slide, click its title or press “Edit”. To make a new one, press “Add slide”.
3. Fill in “Small heading above the title” and “Main heading (h1)”.
4. Write the “Paragraph” (a short sentence or two).
5. Fill in “Button / link text” and “Where the link goes”.
   - For a page on this site type a path such as /about/departments. For another website, paste the full https:// link.
6. Choose the “Background picture” with “Upload a picture”.
   - Landscape pictures at least 1600 pixels wide look best. See the guide Upload pictures and files.
7. Describe the picture in “Picture description (for screen readers)”.
8. Press “Save changes” (or “Create slide” for a new one).

**Good to know**
- Slides rotate in the order shown in the list. To change the order see the guide Re-arrange the order of a list.
- There must always be at least one slide, so the last published slide cannot be archived. Add the new one first, then archive the old one.

### Add or edit a department page

*Anyone who can edit “Departments” · about 15 minutes*

Update a department's description, structure, functions and picture.

1. Press “Departments” in the menu. (`/admin/departments`)
2. To change one, click its name or press “Edit”. For a new one press “Add department”.
3. Type the “Name”, and a “Short description” of one or two sentences (shown on the department cards).
4. Write the “Introduction”.
   - Separate paragraphs with a blank line.
5. Under “Structure, functions and mandate”, press “Add a block” for each section of the page.
   - Each block has a Heading (for example Structure or Functions), an optional Lead-in sentence, and a List with one item per line.
6. Add a “Picture” with “Upload a picture”.
7. Press “Save changes” (or “Create department”).

**Good to know**
- Use “Up” and “Down” on a block to change its order, and “Remove block” to take one out of this page.
- The department's page address is created from its name when it is first added.
- To change the order of departments on the website, see Re-arrange the order of a list.

### Update a leader's profile

*Anyone who can edit “Leadership” · about 8 minutes*

Change a name, title, photo or biography, or show "Name to be announced".

1. Press “Leadership” in the menu. (`/admin/leadership`)
2. Click the person's title or press “Edit”. For someone new press “Add leader”.
3. Check “Role group” (Minister, Permanent Secretary or Director / other) and “Title / position”.
4. Type the “Name”, or leave it empty.
   - While it is empty the website shows “Name to be announced”, which is what to use until a name is confirmed.
5. For the directors list you can set a short “Placeholder label” such as DLS, shown instead of a name.
6. Add a “Photo” if one is approved for publication. Without one, a branded placeholder portrait is shown.
7. Use “Add a detail” under “Biography details” for each line of the profile (for example Born, Education).
8. Press “Save changes”.

**Good to know**
- “Internal note” is for administrators only and is never shown on the website.
- Only publish a photo or biography once the person or the Ministry has approved it.
- Each leader has their own page on the website. Check it with “View on website”.

### Edit The Road Ahead goals

*Anyone who can edit “The Road Ahead” · about 5 minutes*

Change the goals shown on the home page and add confirmed targets under each.

1. Press “The Road Ahead” in the menu. (`/admin/road-ahead`)
2. Click a goal or press “Edit”. For a new goal press “Add goal”.
3. Edit the “Goal” and its “Description”.
4. Press “Add a target” for each measurable target, and fill in “Target” and “Year”.
5. Press “Save changes”.

**Good to know**
- Only add targets the Ministry has confirmed. The website presents them as commitments.
- A goal with no targets simply shows its description.

### Edit the FCSSIP 25 page

*Anyone who can edit “FCSSIP 25 page” · about 10 minutes*

Change the page heading and build the page from paragraphs, headings, links and pictures.

1. Press “FCSSIP 25 page” in the menu. (`/admin/fcssip`)
2. To change the page heading, edit “Title”, “Small heading above the title” and “Introduction under the title”, then press “Save heading”.
3. To change the content, find the block in the list and press “Edit”, or press “Add block” for a new one.
4. Choose the “Type of block”: “Paragraph of text”, “Heading”, “Link in a list” or “Picture”.
   - The type cannot be changed once the block is created. To change it, add a new block and archive the old one.
5. Fill in the fields that appear for that type, then press “Create block” (or “Save changes”).
6. Use “Save new order” after moving blocks up or down so they read top to bottom as you want.

**Good to know**
- Link blocks that sit next to each other appear together as one list.
- For a picture block, give its real width and height in pixels and describe it for screen readers.

### Add or edit an e-service

*Anyone who can edit “E-Services” · about 3 minutes*

Keep the E-Services page and the website footer links up to date.

1. Press “E-Services” in the menu. (`/admin/e-services`)
2. Click a service or press “Edit”. For a new one press “Add service”.
3. Type the “Service name” and a short “Description”.
4. Paste the full https:// address in “Link”.
   - A real address is required. The admin refuses # and anything that is not a proper link.
5. Press “Save changes” (or “Create service”).

**Good to know**
- Services appear on the E-Services page and in the footer of every page. Test the link with “View on website”.

### Publish a document or NCT communique

*Anyone who can edit “Resources & documents” · about 5 minutes*

Add a file to the Resources page.

1. Press “Resources & documents” in the menu. (`/admin/resources`)
2. Press “Add document”.
3. Choose where it appears in “Appears in”: the Resources page, or the NCT Communique list.
4. Type the “Title” and choose its “Category”.
5. Attach the file under “File or link” with “Upload a file”, or paste a full https:// link.
   - PDF, Word (.docx) and Excel (.xlsx) files up to 25 MB are accepted.
6. Press “Create document”.

**Good to know**
- If the file is not ready, leave “File or link” empty. The website shows Available on request with an email link, instead of a Download button that does nothing.
- To add the file later, open the document from the list with “Edit” and upload it.

### Upload pictures and files

*Everyone · about 2 minutes*

Add a picture or document to an item, with the right type and size.

1. Open the item you are editing and find the picture or file field (for example “Picture”, “Background picture”, “Photo” or “File or link”).
2. Press “Upload a picture” (or “Upload a file”) and choose the file from your computer or phone.
   - A small preview appears for pictures when the upload finishes.
3. Alternatively, paste a link into the box next to the preview.
   - Pictures can be linked from Unsplash or Pexels. Files can be linked from any https:// address.
4. Fill in the picture description if the form asks for one, then save the item as usual.

**Good to know**
- Pictures: JPG, PNG, WebP or AVIF, up to 8 MB. Documents: PDF, Word (.docx) or Excel (.xlsx), up to 25 MB.
- Sizes that look best: hero slides, landscape and at least 1600 pixels wide. News and department cards, about 1200 × 750. Leader photos, portrait (4 wide by 5 tall).
- Do not upload pictures you do not have the right to use. Pictures with text in them (such as flyers) need a full description.
- The change is only saved when you save the item. Choosing a file alone does nothing until you press the save button.

**If something goes wrong**
- *It says 'Use a JPG, PNG, WebP or AVIF picture.'* Only those picture types are accepted. SVG and GIF files are not. Open the picture in an editor and save or export it as a JPG or PNG.
- *It says the file is too large.* Pictures must be under 8 MB and documents under 25 MB. Reduce the picture size, or save the document as a smaller PDF.
- *It says 'Uploading isn't set up yet.'* File storage has not been connected. You can still paste a picture link instead. Ask a super administrator to check “System status”.
- *My linked picture is refused.* Only pictures uploaded here and links from Unsplash or Pexels are accepted for pictures.

### Describe a picture for screen readers

*Everyone · about 1 minute*

Write the short description that lets people who cannot see a picture understand it.

1. Find the description field next to the picture, usually “Picture description (for screen readers)”.
2. Write one sentence saying what the picture shows and why it is there.
   - For example: 'Engineers reviewing drawings beside the new rail line', not 'photo1.jpg'.
3. If the picture contains words (a flyer, a chart), include the important words in the description.
4. Save the item.

**Good to know**
- Do not start with 'Picture of' or 'Image of': screen readers already say it is a picture.
- Keep it under about 125 characters unless the picture contains a lot of important text.
- Do not name people unless the Ministry has approved naming them.

### Submit your work for approval

*Everyone · about 3 minutes*

Write something as a draft, send it to a publisher, and see what happens next.

1. Open the section you work in and press the green Add button, or open an item with “Edit”.
2. Fill in the form as usual.
3. Press “Save as draft” to keep it for later, or “Submit for approval” when it is ready.
   - A draft is saved but is not on the website. Submitting sends it to the people who can publish in this section.
4. Look at the Approval panel at the top of the item's page.
   - It says “Draft” or “Waiting for approval”, and what that means for the website.
5. Find your work again from the section's list: drafts are under “Drafts”, submitted work under “Waiting for approval”.
   - Once a publisher approves it, it moves to “Published” and appears on the website.
6. If a publisher sends it back, open it and read the note in the Approval panel. Make the changes and press “Submit for approval” again.
   - Nothing is lost: your work is exactly as you left it, with their note on top.

**Good to know**
- If the button says “Create item” (or the name of that kind of content) instead of “Save as draft”, you can publish in this section, so what you create goes live at once.
- Changing something you already submitted takes it out of the queue until you submit it again.
- To abandon a draft, press “Discard draft”. It is archived, not deleted, and a publisher can restore it.

**If something goes wrong**
- *Nobody has looked at my submission.* Publishers are emailed when you submit (if email is set up), but a quiet word helps. The “Waiting for approval” tab shows it is in the queue.

### Change something that is already on the website

*Everyone · about 3 minutes*

Propose changes to a published item without touching what the public sees.

1. Open the section and press “Edit” beside the item.
2. Make your changes.
3. Press “Save proposed changes”.
   - The website keeps showing the published version. Your changes are kept beside it, and the item is marked “Published · changes in draft”.
4. When they are ready, press “Submit for approval”.
   - A publisher compares your version with what is published, then approves it or sends it back with a note.
5. To drop your changes instead, press “Discard proposed changes” in the Approval panel.
   - The website was never affected, so nothing needs undoing.

**Good to know**
- If you can publish in this section, you will see “Save changes” instead: it puts your version on the website at once.
- When you open the item again, the form shows your proposed version, not the published one. The Approval panel lists exactly what differs.

### Preview how something will look on the website

*Everyone · about 1 minute*

See a draft, or proposed changes, exactly as visitors will see them, before anything is published.

1. Open the item (for example from the “Drafts” tab) and press “Preview” at the top of the page.
   - It opens in a new tab.
2. Look at it as a visitor would, including the pictures and the layout on a narrow screen if you resize the window.
3. An amber bar at the bottom says Preview. Press “Back to editing” when you are done.

**Good to know**
- The preview shows your unpublished words laid over what is live, so it is the version a publisher would approve.
- Only news stories, departments and leaders have a preview, because they are pages of their own. Other content appears inside pages you can already see with “View on website”.
- Nobody can open a preview without signing in, so sending someone the address will not show them anything. Ask a publisher to review it in “Approvals” instead.

### When 'Someone else changed this item' appears

*Everyone*

What to do when two people edit the same item at once.

1. Do not close the page yet. If you typed a lot, select your text and copy it first.
2. Reload the page.
   - You now see the version your colleague saved.
3. Make your change again on top of theirs, and press “Save changes”.

**Good to know**
- This message protects both of you: the admin refuses to overwrite a colleague's work without you seeing it.
- To avoid it, agree who edits an item, and check the “Activity log” (super administrators) to see who edited it last.

## Session 3: Publishing

**For:** Publishers (people who can approve and publish) · **Time:** about 45 minutes

**By the end:** Publishers can review work, approve it or send it back clearly, and keep the website tidy.

**Guides in this session:**

- Approve work, or send it back (3 minutes)
- Preview how something will look on the website (1 minute)
- Edit the Mandate, Mission and Vision (5 minutes)
- Update the contact details and social links (3 minutes)
- Archive something, or bring it back (1 minute)
- Re-arrange the order of a list (2 minutes)

### Approve work, or send it back

*Everyone · about 3 minutes*

For publishers: review what colleagues submitted and decide.

1. Press “Approvals” in the menu. (`/admin/review`)
   - The number beside it is how many items wait for your decision. You only see sections you can publish in.
2. Press “Review” beside an item.
3. Press “Preview” to see how it will look on the website.
   - It opens in a new tab and shows the version waiting for your decision. News stories, departments and leaders have one.
4. Read the Approval panel.
   - For a new item it lists everything that would be published. For a change to something already published it shows each field that differs, with “On the website now” beside “Proposed”.
5. To publish it, press “Approve and publish”.
   - It goes on the website straight away. For a change, the published version is replaced.
6. To send it back, type what needs to change in “Note to the author” and press “Send back”.
   - The website is not changed. The author sees your note when they open the item, fixes it and submits it again.

**Good to know**
- A note is required when you send something back, so the author knows what to fix.
- You can also fix it yourself: change the form below the Approval panel, then press “Publish now” (new item) or “Save changes”.
- You may approve your own work. The “Activity log” records who submitted, approved and sent back each item.

**If something goes wrong**
- *A colleague says they submitted something, but I do not see it.* You only see sections you can publish in. Check they pressed “Submit for approval” (a draft is not submitted yet), or ask a super administrator to check your permissions.

### Preview how something will look on the website

*Everyone · about 1 minute*

See a draft, or proposed changes, exactly as visitors will see them, before anything is published.

1. Open the item (for example from the “Drafts” tab) and press “Preview” at the top of the page.
   - It opens in a new tab.
2. Look at it as a visitor would, including the pictures and the layout on a narrow screen if you resize the window.
3. An amber bar at the bottom says Preview. Press “Back to editing” when you are done.

**Good to know**
- The preview shows your unpublished words laid over what is live, so it is the version a publisher would approve.
- Only news stories, departments and leaders have a preview, because they are pages of their own. Other content appears inside pages you can already see with “View on website”.
- Nobody can open a preview without signing in, so sending someone the address will not show them anything. Ask a publisher to review it in “Approvals” instead.

### Edit the Mandate, Mission and Vision

*Anyone who can edit “Mandate, Mission & Vision” · about 5 minutes*

Change the three statements shown on the home page and the About Us introduction.

1. Press “Mandate, Mission & Vision” in the menu. (`/admin/site-text`)
2. Edit “Mandate”.
   - Shown on the home page as “Why we exist”, and as the introduction on the About Us page.
3. Edit “Vision”.
   - Shown on the home page as “Where we are headed”.
4. Edit “Mission”.
   - Shown on the home page as “How we get there”.
5. Press “Save changes”.
   - All three fields are required. The change is live straight away.

**Good to know**
- Open “View website” afterwards and look at the home page and About Us to see how it reads.
- This text goes live the moment it is saved, so only people who can publish in this section can change it. Others can read it here.

### Update the contact details and social links

*Anyone who can edit “Contact details & links” · about 3 minutes*

Change the address, hours, email, telephone number and social media links shown across the website.

1. Press “Contact details & links” in the menu. (`/admin/contact`)
2. Check “Address” and “Opening hours”.
3. Type the “Email address” people should write to.
   - It is also used for document requests and on the privacy, accessibility and freedom of information pages.
4. Type the “Telephone number”, or leave it empty.
   - An empty number is simply not shown. Only enter a number the Ministry actually answers.
5. Under Social media, check “X (Twitter) link”, “Instagram link”, “Facebook link” and “YouTube link”.
   - Each must start with https://. Leave one empty to hide its icon.
6. Press “Save changes”.
   - The footer of every page and the Contact page update straight away.

**Good to know**
- Open “View website” afterwards and look at the footer and the Contact page.
- These details go live the moment they are saved, so only people who can publish in this section can change them.
- If a colleague saved while you were editing, the admin asks you to reload and try again, so nothing is overwritten.

### Archive something, or bring it back

*Everyone · about 1 minute*

Take an item off the website without deleting it, and restore it later.

1. Open the section that contains the item (for example “News, Photo News & Events”).
2. Press “Archive” beside the item, or open it and press “Archive this item” near the bottom of the page.
3. A box asks you to confirm. Press “Archive” in the box, or “Cancel” to stop.
   - The item disappears from the website straight away. Anyone who has its web address now sees a page not found message.
4. To bring it back, open the “Archived” tab and press “Restore” beside it.
   - It is published again immediately.

**Good to know**
- Nothing is ever deleted. Archived items stay in the “Archived” tab for good, and the “Activity log” records who archived them.
- In lists that can be re-arranged, a restored item rejoins at the end. Move it where you want it with “Up” and “Down”, then press “Save new order”.
- The last published home page hero slide cannot be archived. Add another slide first.
- Archive, Restore and re-arranging change what the website shows, so they need the publish permission. If you cannot see the buttons, ask a publisher.

**If something goes wrong**
- *I cannot find something I archived.* Open the section and press the “Archived” tab. If it is not there, check you are in the right section.

### Re-arrange the order of a list

*Everyone · about 2 minutes*

Decide which hero slide, department, leader, goal, block, service or document comes first.

1. Open a list that can be re-arranged and make sure the “Published” tab is selected.
   - These are Home page hero, Departments, Leadership, The Road Ahead, FCSSIP 25 page, E-Services and Resources & documents.
2. Use the up and down arrow buttons beside an item to move it.
   - Each one is labelled Move the item up or Move the item down for screen readers.
3. When it reads as you want, press “Save new order”.
   - Nothing changes on the website until you press it. While you have moved things, the page says you have unsaved changes to the order.

**Good to know**
- News, Photo News & Events cannot be re-arranged: they are shown newest first.
- Re-ordering is switched off on the “Archived” tab, and while a filter such as Kind or List is selected. Choose “All” first.
- Moving on a phone works the same way: the arrows are large buttons.
- Re-arranging puts a new order on the website at once, so it needs the publish permission for the section.

## Session 4: The enquiries desk

**For:** People who deal with messages from the contact form · **Time:** about 30 minutes

**By the end:** Enquiries are read, answered and closed, and nothing is missed.

**Guides in this session:**

- Read and deal with enquiries (3 minutes)

### Read and deal with enquiries

*Anyone who can edit “Enquiries” · about 3 minutes*

See the messages sent through the website's contact form, reply, and mark them as dealt with.

1. Press “Enquiries” in the menu. (`/admin/enquiries`)
   - The number beside it is how many messages are new.
2. On the “New” tab, press a name to open the message.
3. Read it, then press “Reply by email” to answer from your own email program.
   - Replies go from your mailbox. The website never emails the person who wrote in.
4. Back on the message, type what you did in “Note (optional)” and press “Mark as handled”.
   - It moves to the “Handled” tab, with your name, the time and your note.
5. If it is not a real message, press “Mark as spam” instead.

**Good to know**
- Every message is also emailed to the team for its topic. If a yellow notice says the email did not go out, nobody was told, so check this list regularly until email is working (a super administrator can see “System status”).
- Nothing is deleted. “Handled” and “Spam” messages stay in their tabs, and “Reopen” moves one back to “New”.
- To find an old message, type a name, an email address or a word from the message in the search box and press “Search”.

**If something goes wrong**
- *I am not sure whether a message is spam.* Mark it as spam: you can always press “Reopen” later. A real person who needs an answer will usually write again.

## Session 5: Administering the admin

**For:** Super administrators · **Time:** about 60 minutes

**By the end:** Super administrators can bring people on and off, choose what each can do, route enquiries, and check that everything is connected.

**Guides in this session:**

- Invite someone to the admin (3 minutes)
- Change what someone can manage (2 minutes)
- Correct someone's sign-in email (2 minutes)
- Send an invitation again (1 minute)
- Deactivate or reactivate someone (1 minute)
- Choose who receives each kind of enquiry (3 minutes)
- Read the activity log (2 minutes)
- Check that everything is connected (1 minute)

### Invite someone to the admin

*Super administrators · about 3 minutes*

Give a colleague an account and choose which parts of the website they can change.

1. Press “Users & access” in the menu. (`/admin/users`)
2. In the Invite someone panel, type their “Full name” and “Ministry email address”.
3. Choose their “Role”: “Content editor” or “Super administrator”.
   - A content editor can change only the sections you tick. A super administrator can change everything, including people.
4. Under “Sections this person can manage”, tick “Edit” for the parts of the website they may write drafts in, and “Publish” for the parts they may approve and publish in.
   - Required for content editors: at least one tick. Publishing includes editing. Someone who can only edit has their work reviewed by a publisher first.
5. Press “Send invitation”.
   - They receive an email with a link that works once and lasts 72 hours.

**Good to know**
- If email is not set up yet, the page shows the invitation link once. Copy it and send it to the person yourself, by a channel you trust.
- You can change someone's sections at any time. It takes effect the next time they open a page.
- Give people only the sections they need, and “Publish” to people you trust to approve work. Make sure every section has at least one publisher.
- “Enquiries” has a single tick, “Edit”: it lets the person read and deal with the messages from the contact form.

**If something goes wrong**
- *It says to choose at least one section.* A content editor needs at least one ticked section. Tick one, or make them a super administrator if they should manage everything.

### Change what someone can manage

*Super administrators · about 2 minutes*

Add or remove sections, rename a person, or change their role.

1. Press “Users & access”, then “Manage” beside the person. (`/admin/users`)
2. In the Access panel, change “Full name”, “Role” or the ticked sections.
   - “Edit” lets them write drafts and propose changes. “Publish” lets them approve work, archive and re-arrange.
3. Press “Save access”.
   - It takes effect the next time they load a page.

**Good to know**
- The last active super administrator cannot be demoted or deactivated, so the admin can never be left without one.

### Correct someone's sign-in email

*Super administrators · about 2 minutes*

Fix a mistyped address, or move a person to a new email, without losing their account.

1. Press “Users & access”, then “Manage” beside the person. (`/admin/users`)
2. In the Sign-in email panel, type the new address in “Sign-in email address”.
3. Press “Change email address”.
   - Their password, access and history stay as they are. If they are already active, both the old and the new address get an email saying it changed.

**Good to know**
- If the person has not activated yet, a new invitation is sent to the new address and the old link stops working. That is the fix for an invitation sent to a mistyped address.
- You cannot change your own address, so a typing mistake cannot lock you out. Ask another super administrator.
- An address already used by another account is refused.

### Send an invitation again

*Super administrators · about 1 minute*

Replace a lost or expired invitation link.

1. Press “Users & access”. People who have not activated yet show as Invited. (`/admin/users`)
2. Press “Manage” beside them, then “Send a new invitation” in the Account panel.
   - The previous link stops working.

**Good to know**
- If they still do not get the email, check “System status” to see whether email is connected.

### Deactivate or reactivate someone

*Super administrators · about 1 minute*

Stop a person signing in (for example when they leave), without losing their history.

1. Press “Users & access”, then “Manage” beside the person. (`/admin/users`)
2. In the Account panel, press “Deactivate account”.
   - They are signed out everywhere and cannot sign in. Their entries in the “Activity log” stay.
3. To let them back in, open the same page and press “Reactivate account”.

**Good to know**
- You cannot deactivate your own account here, and the last active super administrator is protected. People are never deleted.

### Choose who receives each kind of enquiry

*Super administrators · about 3 minutes*

Send messages from the contact form to the right team's email address.

1. Press “Enquiries” in the menu, then “Where enquiries go”. (`/admin/enquiries/settings`)
2. For each kind of message, type the email address of the team that should receive it.
3. Leave a box empty to use the Ministry's main email address.
   - That address is set under “Contact details & links”.
4. Press “Save changes”.
   - It applies to messages sent after you save.

**Good to know**
- Use a shared team mailbox rather than one person's, so enquiries are not missed when someone is away.
- Every message is also kept in “Enquiries”, whichever address it was emailed to.
- Email must be set up for these notifications to be sent. See “System status”.

### Read the activity log

*Super administrators · about 2 minutes*

See who signed in, changed, archived or restored what, and when.

1. Press “Activity log” in the menu. (`/admin/audit`)
2. Read across: when it happened, who did it, the action, and the details.
3. Press “Older →” at the bottom to go back in time, and “← Newer” to return.
   - Each page shows 50 entries.

**Good to know**
- The log cannot be edited or deleted by anyone, including super administrators.

### Check that everything is connected

*Super administrators · about 1 minute*

See whether the database, email and file uploads are working.

1. Press “System status” in the menu. (`/admin/status`)
2. Read each line: Database, Content seeded, Email, File uploads and Website address.
   - A green dot is fine. An amber dot means something needs attention, and the line says what.
3. Under Content, check the counts. They include archived items.

**Good to know**
- Secret values (passwords, keys) are never shown here. Ask the technical team to fix anything marked amber.

## Words used in the admin

- **Activity log**: A permanent record of who signed in, changed, archived or restored what, and when. Only super administrators can read it, and nobody can edit it.
- **Archive**: Hide something from the website without deleting it. Archived items stay in the Archived tab and can be restored at any time.
- **Content editor**: A person who can change only the sections of the website a super administrator has given them.
- **Event**: An entry on the Events page for something happening on a date, such as a council meeting, with a place and a short summary.
- **Enquiry**: A message someone sent through the contact form on the website. It is emailed to the right team and kept in the Enquiries section until someone marks it as handled.
- **Feature (an item)**: Mark a news item so it is highlighted on the home page.
- **Hero**: The large picture-and-headline carousel at the top of the home page. Each picture with its words is a slide.
- **Invitation**: The email that lets a new person choose a password and start using the admin. The link works once and lasts 72 hours.
- **Kind**: For news items, whether it is online news, photo news or an event. It decides which page of the website it appears on.
- **Approval**: A publisher's decision on work an editor submitted. Approved work goes on the website; work sent back returns to its author with a note.
- **Draft**: A new item that is saved but not on the website yet. It goes live only when a publisher approves it (or a publisher publishes it).
- **Live**: Visible on the public website. A publisher's saved changes go live straight away; an editor's wait for approval.
- **Proposed changes**: Edits to something already published, saved beside it. The website keeps the published version until a publisher approves them.
- **Publisher**: A person who has the Publish permission for a section: they can approve others' work, put changes on the website, archive and re-arrange. Super administrators can publish everywhere.
- **Photo news**: A news item built around a photograph, shown on the Photo News page.
- **Picture description**: A short sentence saying what a picture shows, read aloud to people who use a screen reader and shown if the picture cannot load. Also called alt text.
- **Placeholder**: Words shown while the real information is not ready, for example Name to be announced in place of a leader's name.
- **Press release**: An official statement from the Ministry. In the admin it is an online news item with the press release box ticked.
- **Published**: Shown on the website now. The opposite of archived.
- **Section**: A part of the website you can manage, such as News or Departments. Content editors are given sections one by one.
- **Session**: The period you stay signed in, 12 hours. The admin warns you shortly before it ends.
- **Super administrator**: A person who can change everything: all sections, plus people, the activity log and system status.
- **Unsaved changes**: Words or choices you have typed that have not been saved yet. The admin shows a note and asks before you leave.
